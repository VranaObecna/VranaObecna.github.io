My path in technology

Why IT?

The motivation behind my choice, how I plan to grow, and what I want to create.

01 / WHY

Why?

I chose ICT because I’ve always been fascinated by technology and where it’s heading (Probably same as everyone else in this programme :D). It all starts with a simple curiosity about how computers actually work. It's crazy to think how humans managed to build something as complex as AI out of practically sticks and stones. That fascination quickly turns into an endless puzzle: I find myself sitting behind a monitor for hours, fueled by caffeine, trying to fix non-functioning code. When it finally works, I get pure joy for about five minutes—right before I realize I just created ten new bugs to solve. That chaotic cycle of breaking things and fixing them is honestly what keeps me hooked.

02 / HOW

How?

I’m going to achieve my goals by actually showing up to lectures, listening to teachers who have already made the same mistakes I'm making right now, and putting in the hours. Of course, it’s not just about school. It’s also about countless late nights watching tutorials by random experts on YouTube who somehow know everything I need to figure out. I'm learning by experimenting, failing, and pushing through the frustration until the code finally runs.

03 / WHAT

What?

I study HBO-ICT at HZ University of Applied Sciences, and I'm working towards becoming a solid developer who can build real, working software. My focus is on game development, writing clean code, and mastering the skills needed to turn complex ideas into actual playable realities.
