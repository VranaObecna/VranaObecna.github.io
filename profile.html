Who am I?

Writes clean code - loves the challenge of development - pushes through until the project is mastered.

I'm Thomas, 19 year old ICT student at HZ University of Applied Sciences, driven by a deep fascination for software development, game creation, and cybersecurity. I’m someone who loves to dive straight into building things and figuring out how technology works under the hood.

Outside of my studies, I regularly hit the gym after classes to recharge, and on weekends you’ll often find me relaxing with TV shows like The Mentalist or Dr. House, or just going for a drive. I used to play basketball and various other sports actively, which taught me the value of teamwork and focus. My strength lies in my passion for programming and problem solving.

I enjoy seeing a project come together from concept to a working product.

A fact nobody would guess about me: underneath my casual approach, I am a total perfectionist when it comes to the things I truly care about and enjoy.

  • From: Košice, Slovakia
  • Hobbies: Gaming, Music, Traveling, Gym, Coding
  • Favorite movie: Interstellar

My digital interests

I’m especially interested in the game development field and I already have some experience with it. Websites aren’t my strong point and I have never really been interested in them, but I slowly started to like them and I want to learn more about them. I’m also someone who wants to know how things works, so I’m interested in learning about everything that is related to computers and technology in general.
